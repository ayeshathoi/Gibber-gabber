For a long time, I wished for a second chance at everything. And perhaps, this is my second chance.

Sometimes, we have to lose our sense of direction before we learn to find our way again. We become so occupied with everything around us that we forget to make ourselves a priority. And sometimes, it takes losing almost everything to finally understand what it means to rise again.

It's ironic and sad. But perhaps there is some beauty in finally finding yourself above all the things that once held you down.

The Almighty has a way of opening doors when we have convinced ourselves that none remain.

"And whoever is mindful of Allah, He will make a way out for them and provide for them from sources they could never imagine." [65:2–3]

There was a time when even the strongest convictions seemed insufficient to make sense of life. When every possible way forward seemed to disappear, and the thought of continuing the struggle became almost unbearable. Perhaps that is what hopelessness does: it makes the temporary feel permanent and the impossible seem inevitable.

And yet,

"For indeed, with hardship comes ease." [94:5]

There are experiences that change the way we think, perceive, and navigate the world. Some leave marks that cannot easily be explained, and some are better left unnamed. 

Perhaps not everyone will understand the extent of what another person has endured. But suffering is not a competition, and everyone's struggles deserve compassion.

Some of life's most difficult lessons come from discovering the difference between what people say and what they are willing to do. Presence is easy to promise; showing up when things become difficult is another matter entirely.

Yet, the most important thing is not who stayed or who left. It is that, somewhere along the way, the desire to keep going survived.

And perhaps that is what a second chance really means: not returning to the person you once were, but finding your way toward someone you never thought you would become.

Nowadays, I find myself thinking more clearly, feeling more like myself, and recognizing glimpses of the person I remember.

And honestly, that itself is a blessing. 

Many things in life seem like curses at first, only to reveal themselves as blessings in disguise.
