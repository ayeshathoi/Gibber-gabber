# Questions About Human Existence, Meaning, God, and Hell

### Human existence and its purpose

1. **What is the purpose of a human life?**

2. **What is the purpose of human existence? Why are we here?**

3. **Is there an objective purpose to human life, independent of what humans want or believe?**

4. **Do humans actually need to accomplish anything with their lives?**

5. **What if we don't need a purpose at all?**

6. **What if humans are simply existing, without any particular mission or obligation?**

7. **Is human life actually meaningful?**

8. **What if humans are not capable of knowing the real purpose of their existence?**

9. **Why does anything exist at all?**

10. **Why does the universe contain conscious beings capable of asking why the universe exists?**

11. **Why are humans conscious enough to question their own existence, their Creator, and eternity?**

### Religion and the purpose of humanity

12. **What does every religion say about the purpose of human life?**

13. **Do the major religions actually provide an objective explanation for why humans exist?**

14. **If God created humans to worship Him, why did He create beings who need to be told why they were created?**

15. **If God is already perfect and self-sufficient, why would God create humans at all?**

16. **Why would God create beings who can suffer, question Him, and even reject Him?**

17. **Why is human existence structured this way—with birth, childhood, aging, suffering, desire, attachment, and death?**

18. **Why didn't God create humans who already understood everything?**

### The universe and human significance

19. **What if there is nothing, and we are all galaxy?**

20. **What if humans, Earth, galaxies, and everything else are simply different manifestations of one existence?**

21. **Are humans actually important in the universe?**

22. **If the universe existed long before humans and will probably continue after humans disappear, does that make human existence meaningless?**

23. **If humanity is cosmically insignificant, does that mean our lives are meaningless?**

24. **Why should existence need to justify itself?**

25. **A galaxy doesn't need to justify its existence—why should a human being?**

### Suffering and Hell

26. **If every possible bad thing has happened to someone, have they already experienced Hell?**

27. **If someone has experienced every possible form of human suffering, what could Hell possibly add?**

28. **If there are no unique incidents happening in Hell, how can Hell be the greatest suffering?**

29. **If Hell is supposed to be the greatest possible suffering, what makes it greater than the worst suffering that can already happen on Earth?**

30. **If Hell is just the same suffering forever, is Hell actually worse because of intensity, or only because of duration?**

31. **If someone's life is suffering and they don't know when it will be over, is that not essentially the same as an eternal Hell from their subjective perspective?**

32. **If a person does not know whether their suffering will end tomorrow, in ten years, or never, how is that fundamentally different from being told that suffering will never end?**

33. **Does infinite duration itself make suffering infinitely worse?**

34. **If a person has already experienced unimaginable suffering during life, what new kind of suffering could Hell provide?**

35. **If Hell contains suffering that humans can already experience on Earth, what makes Hell categorically different?**

36. **If Hell is eternal, what exactly does “eternal suffering” mean from the perspective of someone who can only experience one moment at a time?**

37. **If someone eventually leaves Hell, how can Hell be described as never-ending?**

38. **If God saves someone who is in Hell, then how can their punishment be eternal?**

39. **If God ultimately saves people from Hell, what exactly is Hell supposed to accomplish?**

40. **If God is perfectly merciful, perfectly just, and capable of saving people, why would He allow anyone to suffer eternally?**

41. **If earthly suffering can already be unimaginably terrible, why would God need to create an afterlife with even greater suffering?**

42. **Can infinite punishment ever be proportionate to finite human actions?**

43. **If human beings live for a finite amount of time, why would their punishment need to last forever?**

44. **What would make eternal punishment morally or philosophically different from temporary punishment?**
