1. What is the ultimate purpose of human existence? Why are we here?

2. How do different religions explain the purpose of human life? What do they believe humans were created for?

3. Is human life inherently meaningful, or is meaning something we construct ourselves?

4. Do human beings actually need to accomplish anything during their lifetime? What if existence itself requires no particular purpose or achievement?

5. What if there is no greater purpose behind our existence? What if we are simply a small part of an enormous universe, no more significant than the galaxies surrounding us?

6. If someone has already experienced every conceivable form of human suffering during their lifetime, could they be said to have already experienced Hell?

7. If Hell does not involve forms of suffering fundamentally different from those humans can experience on Earth, what makes it the greatest form of suffering?

8. If someone spends their entire life suffering without knowing when, or whether, their suffering will end, how is that experience fundamentally different from the suffering associated with an eternal Hell?

9. If God eventually saves those who are in Hell, how can Hell be described as a place of eternal or never-ending punishment?
