I think I’m a little crazy. And most likely, I could have done everything I’ve ever wanted to do. But I’ve always been more inclined to learn than to practice. I tend to become obsessed with the things I want to do, and that obsession sometimes makes me seem a little crazy.

A few years ago, I was quite sad because I thought I had lost that obsessiveness for good. But it seems to have returned, and now I feel like I can be as crazy as I want to be. I used to feel a little embarrassed when people started realizing just how crazy I really am. I tried to hide that side of myself. But now that I’ve been an adult for a while and no longer have to depend on anyone else, I feel much more at peace with people knowing about my craziness.

Besides, I come from a strong line of lunatics, so my craziness might be genetic, too. Maybe that’s why I’m so unpredictable. But I’ve decided to wear all of it as a crown.

Sometimes, I feel sad thinking about what I could have become and all the things I might have done. I believe in people’s potential, and perhaps even more importantly, I believe in my own. But I also know that every time I choose to excel at one thing, I inevitably give something else up.

But maybe that’s just what being human means. And I’m human, too. We’re all giving up something to gain something else. We can’t become every version of ourselves that we could have been, no matter how much potential we have. And perhaps the hardest part is never knowing how those other versions of ourselves might have turned out. We don’t even get to try being them. We simply don’t have enough time.
