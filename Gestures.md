I have a pretty good memory, and I can remember almost everything. Still, there are times when I forget things.

These notes are a way of keeping track of the good things that have happened to me, because my mind tends to wander back to the bad ones.

---

One day, I was at UMass Dining. With a meal plan card, we could eat pretty much anything there. However, it was getting late, and it was time to head home.

There was an old man standing in front of me with a plate full of watermelon, separate from his original plate. He had two plates.

Suddenly, I found myself craving watermelon. But I couldn't get any because I was running out of time. It was almost time for my bus, and the buses came only once an hour. If I missed that one, I'd have to wait another hour to get home. It was already 9 p.m.

Then, out of nowhere, the old man offered me some watermelon from his plate. It's not that I had been staring at his plate or that he had noticed me looking at it. He simply offered, very naturally.

I know it might seem like a silly little thing, but it was so unexpected. I genuinely appreciated that small gesture.

Sometimes, it's the smallest acts of kindness that stay with us.
