
I believe, to some extent, that people eventually get what they are meant to get. 
“What’s meant for you will reach you even if it’s beneath two mountains, and what’s not meant for you won’t reach you even if it’s between your two lips.” 

But perhaps everything we go through is also a test; a way of revealing who we truly are.

Sometimes, we look at someone and think they have fallen so far that they could never rise again. But the truth is, the way we treat them in that moment reveals far more about us than it does about them. The person we look down on may or may not rise to greatness anytime soon. That is not the point. 

"It is our choices, Harry, that show what we truly are, far more than our abilities."


There was something that I was very confused about: Why has the Almighty sent us into this world to create chaos if He has already written our destinies?
But one day, one of my classmates said something really important that caught my attention. The Almighty knows us so well that He knows what we are going to do. And that is what He has written.
Perhaps this is not true. I have very little knowledge of religion. As the days pass, I tend to forget more and more of what I used to know.
But this is something that sounds fair to me. Otherwise, it felt so unjust.
