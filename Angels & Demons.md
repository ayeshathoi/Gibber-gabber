How does heaven actually work? What would make people so happy there if everything were already achieved and we didn't need to work for anything?

And is the Lord so bored with the people in hell that He would let their punishments continue in a loop? I read that the punishments would repeat over and over again, with the same things happening again and again.

I believe in heaven and hell, but I find it quite confusing that people would be so happy in heaven if they got everything all at once. Isn't our desire for something dependent on the fact that we can't have it without hardship? We always praise light because there is darkness. Or would the laws and our mentality be different there?


If so, how would people remain the same people they were on Earth? Have they suppressed their desires for so long that they would need an eternity to fill that?

Or is the knowledge of hell, and the awareness that they have been spared from it, part of what makes the people in heaven feel such profound happiness and gratitude?



I don't know if these are the right questions to think about.

And again, I believe we cannot claim that we will attain heaven simply because we have done everything right, as that would be a sign of pride. After all, we are not angels, and we are bound to make mistakes. Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde.

Ultimately, it is only through the Almighty’s mercy that we may attain it. Everything we receive on this Earth is already a blessing in itself.
